Privacy Policy

Boat Pro USA, operated by Captain Ron, respects your privacy. This policy explains information collected through BoatProUSA.com and the newsletter service.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, signup source, consent record, subscription status, email-delivery status, and unsubscribe requests. Payment providers may separately collect payment information; Boat Pro USA should not store full card numbers.

How information is used

Information is used to deliver requested guides, send newsletters and topic emails, administer memberships, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, keep operational records, and improve the service.

Service providers

Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Google Drive, email-delivery services, website hosting, analytics tools, and Stripe may process information on our behalf when those services are enabled.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers, to comply with law, to protect rights and safety, or during a legitimate business transfer.

Retention and security

Records are retained as reasonably necessary for subscriber service, legal compliance, accounting, dispute resolution, and abuse prevention. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe using the link in an email or contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. Some records may be retained when legally required.
